# Privacy Policy — Japan Explorer

**Effective date:** 2026-06-08  
**Developer:** Funvestment  
**App:** Japan Explorer (com.funvestment.japan_explorer)

---

## 1. Introduction

Japan Explorer ("the App", "we", "us") is a travel and cultural discovery application for visitors to Japan. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

By using Japan Explorer, you agree to this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Display name** | Shown on your profile and community posts |
| **Email address** | Account creation, sign-in, support |
| **Profile photo** (optional) | Shown on your profile and community posts |
| **Photos you upload** | Attached to community posts about spots |
| **Post content** (reviews, ratings, tags) | Published to the community spot page |

### 2.2 Automatically Collected

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Location** (when in use) | Show nearby spots on the map; AI landmark identification |
| **Camera images** | AI landmark identification (processed by Google Gemini — images are not stored by us) |
| **Device identifiers** | Firebase Analytics, push notification delivery (FCM) |
| **Usage data** (screens visited, features used) | Firebase Analytics — app improvement |
| **Crash reports** | Firebase Crashlytics — bug detection and fixing |

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

- Financial or payment information
- Health or fitness data
- Contacts or address book
- Browsing history outside the App
- Precise background location (we use "when in use" only)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Provide core features**: map, spot discovery, AI camera, community posts
- **Personalize your experience**: streak tracking, badge unlocks, recommended spots
- **Send push notifications**: streak reminders, badge achievements (opt-out in device settings)
- **Improve the App**: analytics and crash reports
- **Moderate community content**: review posts for quality and safety

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- User data is stored in **Firebase Firestore** and **Firebase Storage**, hosted on Google Cloud.
- Data is stored in the **asia-northeast1 (Tokyo)** region where possible.
- User-uploaded photos are stored in Firebase Storage. Only approved posts are publicly visible.
- Passwords are never stored by us — authentication is handled by Firebase Auth.
- We use HTTPS/TLS for all data transmission.

---

## 6. Community Content (UGC)

Posts you submit (photos, ratings, reviews) go through a moderation queue before being publicly visible.

- **Public posts**: visible to all app users once approved.
- **Deletion**: you may delete your own posts at any time from the App.
- Severely violating content may be removed by moderators without notice.

---

## 7. AI Camera Feature

When you use the AI camera:
- Your image is sent to **Google Gemini API** for landmark identification.
- Images are used only to generate the AI response and are **not stored** by Japan Explorer or Gemini beyond that request (subject to Google's data retention policies).
- Location data is not automatically attached to camera images.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Japan Explorer is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Export** your data
- **Opt out** of analytics (disable in device settings via "Limit Ad Tracking")

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

## 10. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data:
1. Open Japan Explorer → Profile → Settings → Delete Account
2. Or email us at the address below with subject "Account Deletion Request"

Upon deletion, your profile, posts, streak data, and badges are permanently removed within 30 days.
