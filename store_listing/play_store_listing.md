## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
Discover Japan like never before with Japan Explorer — your AI-powered cultural companion for every step of your Japanese adventure.

🌸 EXPLORE WITH AI
Point your camera at any shrine, temple, garden, or street scene and let our AI instantly identify it, tell its story, and suggest what to experience nearby. Powered by Google Gemini.

🗺️ CURATED SPOTS
Browse hundreds of hand-curated cultural spots across Japan — from iconic Tokyo landmarks to hidden Kyoto gems. Filter by category (temples, food, festivals, nature, and more) and save the ones you love to your personal plan.

📸 COMMUNITY EXPERIENCES
See real photos and stories from travelers who've been there. Share your own experiences, rate spots, and add tips to help the next visitor.

🏆 CULTURAL JOURNEY SYSTEM
Earn XP and badges as you explore, learn, and contribute. Build your daily streak, unlock cultural levels, and collect achievement badges that reflect your growing knowledge of Japan.

📍 MAP & NEARBY
Open the interactive map to see curated spots around you. Tap any pin for details, photos, ratings, and AI insights — all while walking through Japan's streets.

✨ KEY FEATURES
• AI landmark recognition via camera
• 500+ curated cultural spots
• Community reviews & photo sharing
• Interactive map with nearby discovery
• Daily streak & badge achievement system
• Multilingual AI travel guide (English focus)
• Offline-friendly spot cards
• Save spots to your personal trip plan
• Free to use

Japan Explorer is designed for international visitors who want to go beyond the tourist trail and connect with Japanese culture on a deeper level.

Download free and start exploring Japan today! 🎋
```
